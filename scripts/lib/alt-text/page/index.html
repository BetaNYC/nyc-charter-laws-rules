Picture descriptions

Text descriptions for the pictures in New York City's Administrative Code and Rules. They are written by BetaNYC: an AI model drafts each one, and a person reviews it. They are not part of American Legal Publishing's text and are not an official record.

Save writes your review to data/alt-text/ in this copy of the repository.

See something wrong or missing? Write a better description and press "Suggest a better description". It opens a new GitHub issue with your text filled in; nothing is sent until you submit it there, and a person at BetaNYC reviews every suggestion.

See something wrong or missing? Write a better description and press "Send suggestion". You do not need an account. Your suggestion goes to BetaNYC, not to a public page, and a person at BetaNYC reads every one before anything on the Law Reader changes.
